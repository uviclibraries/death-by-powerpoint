# Record Your Presentation (OPTIONAL)

Please record your presentation using either Google Vids or Zoom screen sharing. Google video allows you to record and edit your screen cast, while Zoom screen sharing allows you to record your screencast but not edit it.

## Google Vids
- Here is the [Google Vids screen recording activity](https://uviclibraries.github.io/video-editing/screen-recording.html){:target="_blank"} from our Video Editing workshop.

## Zoom Screen Sharing
- Start a Zoom call just for yourself
- Share your screen (but not video)
- Start recording your presentation (using Zoom)
- Stop recording when you're done and after you end the Zoom call, Zoom will generate a video of your presentation that you can share if you'd like.
